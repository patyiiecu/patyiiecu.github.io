*Vertical lines, parallel lines, perpendicular lines.*
A line is vertical if it is parallel to the vertical axis, i.e.,
its equation is of the form stem:[x=x_0=const].
Two lines are parallel if and only if they have the same slope.
Two lines are perpendicular if and only if their slopes are
negative reciprocals of each other.
